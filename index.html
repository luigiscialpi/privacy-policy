Privacy Policy - WhatStat

Last updated: 2026-06-09

1. About Us

This Privacy Policy describes how WhatStat processes data during the use of the app.

2. Key Principle of the App

WhatStat analyzes WhatsApp data locally on the device. Chat content (text messages), phone numbers, JIDs, and contact names are not sent from the app’s tracking mechanisms to external servers.

3. Processed Data and Third-Party Services

The app integrates Google/Firebase services that may process technical data necessary for operation, analytics, advertising, and diagnostics.

  • Firebase Analytics: technical app usage events (e.g., screens viewed, clicks, app version, aggregated technical parameters).
  • Firebase Remote Config: remote configurations (e.g., feature flags, forced updates, warning messages).
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and diagnostics (stack trace, app version, device model, technical runtime status).
  • Google AdMob: advertising and fraud prevention; may process advertising/device identifiers and technical data.
  • Google UMP (User Messaging Platform): ad consent management, where required.

4. Data Categories (High Level)

  • App interactions (app usage events)
  • Device/other identifiers (technical/advertising identifiers)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data (stability and bug fixing)

5. Purposes of processing

  • Analytics and product improvement
  • Application features (runtime configurations, forced updates)
  • Advertising (free version)
  • Security/stability (crash diagnostics)

6. What we DO NOT collect in our app events

  • No message text/chat content
  • No phone numbers
  • No contact JIDs
  • No contact names from the chat database

7. Retention and control

Retention periods may depend on third-party services (Google/Firebase). Users can manage advertising preferences and consent according to the options available on their device and in Google services.

8. Security

Data transmitted to the cloud services used by the app is protected in transit in accordance with the provider’s standards.

9. Children

The app is not specifically intended for children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated over time. Changes will be posted on this page.
